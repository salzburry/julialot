Regarding Melphalan, can we look at the impact of applying this rule:

If the first MELP dose is inside the induction window:
  - Next dose <180 days later  -> doesn't advance the LOT.
  - Next dose >=180 days later -> advances the LOT (new LOT starts at
    that later dose).

If the first MELP dose is outside the induction window:
  - Next dose <60 days later     -> advances the LOT, but the LOT
    actually starts back at the FIRST dose (so that first dose becomes
    day 0 of the new LOT).
  - Next dose 60-180 days later  -> doesn't advance the LOT (both doses
    stay in the current line).
  - Next dose >=180 days later   -> doesn't advance for the first dose,
    but the later dose (>=180 days) starts a new LOT on its own date.
